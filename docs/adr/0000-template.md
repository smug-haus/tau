## Context

What's the situation that needs a decision? Be specific. Name the
files, the constraints, and the forces in tension. A reader who
opens this ADR a year from now should understand the world as it
was when the decision was made, without having to read the diff.

## Decision

The chosen course, stated as a single declarative sentence, then
expanded with the specifics. Use _present_ tense ("Tau loads X
once at boot"), not future tense.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision. Include both the upsides (what
this enables) and the costs (what it forecloses). Be honest about
the tradeoffs.

## Alternatives considered

Two or three real alternatives, each with one or two sentences on
why it was rejected. "We considered no alternatives" is rarely
true; if you genuinely didn't, say so explicitly.

## Notes

Optional. Anything that doesn't fit above: links to discussions,
benchmarks, prior art, or things to revisit if a specific
condition changes.
